# Caffeine Privacy Policy / Caffeine 隐私政策

Last updated November 10, 2026 / 更新于 2026 年 11 月 10 日

## English

Caffeine does not collect, transmit, or share personal data. It has no account, analytics, advertising, or tracking. Its sleep controls run locally through your operating system.

The app reads your operating system’s language setting for the automatic language option. It saves your language choice and last active awake mode in a local `settings.json` file. The Mac App Store edition stores this file in its app container. Removing the local settings file resets these preferences.

When you choose **Privacy Policy** in the app, your default browser opens this page on GitHub. GitHub handles that visit under its own privacy statement. The app does not send your preferences or awake status to GitHub. [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)

For privacy questions, contact the developer through [Caffeine Support](support.md).

## 简体中文

Caffeine 不收集、传输或共享个人数据。应用无需账号，不含分析统计、广告或跟踪功能。防休眠操作通过设备上的操作系统接口完成。

应用读取操作系统的语言设置，用于「跟随系统」选项。应用仅在本机的 `settings.json` 中保存语言偏好和上次启用的模式。Mac App Store 版将该文件保存在应用沙盒内。删除本地设置文件可重置这些偏好。

在应用内点击「隐私政策」时，默认浏览器会打开 GitHub 上的本页。GitHub 根据自己的隐私声明处理此次访问。应用不会将偏好或防休眠状态发送给 GitHub。 [GitHub 隐私声明](https://docs.github.com/zh/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)

如有隐私问题，可通过 [Caffeine 支持页面](support.md) 联系开发者。
