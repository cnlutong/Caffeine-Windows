# Caffeine Support / Caffeine 支持

Open Caffeine and find the coffee cup in the menu bar on Mac or the notification area on Windows. Click the cup to toggle your last active mode. Right-click or Control-click it for modes, Settings, and Quit.

For help, contact the maintainer at [Caffeine GitHub Issues](https://github.com/cnlutong/Caffeine-Windows/issues). Include your Caffeine version, operating system version, and steps to reproduce the issue.

在 Mac 上从菜单栏、在 Windows 上从通知区域找到咖啡杯。单击可切换上次启用的模式。右键单击或在 Mac 上按住 Control 单击，可以选择模式、打开设置或退出。

如需帮助，请在 [Caffeine GitHub Issues](https://github.com/cnlutong/Caffeine-Windows/issues) 联系维护者。请附上应用版本、系统版本和复现步骤。
